# ✈️ TripGenius AI – Smart Travel Itinerary Planner

TripGenius AI is a Flutter-based travel planning application designed to help users organize their trips easily. Users can enter their destination, travel duration, budget, interests, and preferred travel style to generate a personalized day-wise travel itinerary.

The application provides a simple, interactive, and user-friendly interface to make trip planning easier and more organized.

## 🌟 Features

- 🗺️ **Destination Planning** – Enter your preferred travel destination.
- 📅 **Flexible Duration** – Choose the number of days for your trip.
- 💰 **Budget-Based Planning** – Set your travel budget.
- 🎯 **Interest Selection** – Choose interests such as nature, history, food, and adventure.
- 🚗 **Travel Style Selection** – Select budget-friendly, comfort, or luxury travel.
- 🗓️ **Day-Wise Itinerary** – View planned morning, afternoon, and evening activities.
- 💡 **Travel Tips** – Get helpful tips for your trip.
- 📋 **Itinerary Summary** – View your selected preferences and planned activities.
- 🔄 **Reset Option** – Reset the form and create another travel plan.
- 📤 **Shareable Preview** – View a summary of your itinerary for sharing.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
